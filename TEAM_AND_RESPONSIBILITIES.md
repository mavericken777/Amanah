## Responsibility rule

Every material task must have:
- one accountable owner;
- a due date or triggering gate;
- a completion condition;
- evidence;
- a current state.

## Escalation

Operational issue → accountable owner → Mission lead  
Commercial/legal mandate issue → relevant authorised lead → Executive sponsor  
Authority/certification issue → competent-authority workflow  
Critical safety/travel issue → approved emergency procedure
