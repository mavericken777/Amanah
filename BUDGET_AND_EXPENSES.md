## Expense record

Each expense record should include:

- date;
- city;
- category;
- description;
- amount;
- currency;
- payer / cost centre;
- reimbursable status;
- receipt or invoice reference;
- approval status.

Keep payment-card numbers, bank credentials, private booking credentials and sensitive personal information outside the repository.
