roles

Who does what

PHC

Perak Halal Corporation is the Perak State Government halal-industry GLC in the project model, serving local and international industry development. The proposed institutional workflow includes PHC coordination with authorized human authority actors; it does not grant PHC independent certification powers.

GHSCL HK

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited Hong Kong is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle connecting the Malaysian institutional layer, China participants and GCC destination interfaces. JV ownership terms require supporting corporate instruments.

AHTE

The Amanah Halal Trust Ecosystem links standards intelligence, applicability, evidence, audits, digital twins, monitoring, integrity, trust state and exception orchestration. It does not act as the certification authority.

JAKIM / MAIN / JAIN

Competent Malaysian certification authorities retain decisions within their mandates. Mufti, scholars and designated human reviewers participate only within an evidenced, authorized decision workflow.

China origin ecosystem

Sources, producers, suppliers, manufacturers, laboratories, warehouses, logistics and ports contribute purpose-bound evidence. Exact laboratory identity, accreditation and recognition are separate validation objects.

GCC destination ecosystem

Authorities, customs, importers, receiving warehouses, distributors, retailers and buyers apply destination controls. Origin assurance does not guarantee destination acceptance.

Authority connectivity is an interface

JAKIM is connected through a secure Authority Gateway, alongside independent operational participants. The diagram describes exchange boundaries, not corporate ownership or a currently connected official API.

Technical detail

The gateway boundary covers authenticated exchange, schema validation, case correlation, integrity verification, retry/idempotency, audit logs and signed status updates. Official endpoint, authentication, mandate and data contract remain source-locked.

human

Approval and disapproval remain human decisions

The designed workflow routes a complete audit and evidence dossier to authorized human decision-makers. Approval, disapproval, conditions, expiry, suspension and withdrawal must originate from an authenticated authority record; a platform score cannot manufacture that record.

Technical detail

The handoff requests a PHC/JAKIM Mufti/scholar certification workflow. The canonical partner registry assigns PHC a commercial industry role and explicitly reserves official certification to JAKIM/MAIN/JAIN. That mandate conflict is recorded in CLAIMS_AND_GATES_2026-09-30.json and remains open.
