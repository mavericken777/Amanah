platform suite

The complete platform, connected end to end

Amanah is the operational workspace. AHTE provides the trust and assurance layer. GHSCL’s international operating model connects participating organizations across the journey. Together, the platform brings everyday coordination and supply-chain assurance into one coherent experience.

Enterprise and facility profiles, products and SKUs, formulas and BOMs, suppliers, origin records, training and remediation from onboarding through continuous assurance.

Standards intelligence

Complete applicable Malaysian/JAKIM standards and instruments are mapped by applicability to requirements, controls, HCP/SCCP obligations and source-bound evidence; MS2400 is one applicable standards family, not the complete framework.

authority

A direct path for authority-connected assurance

A direct connection keeps the authority workflow clear: AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM. Formal certification approval or disapproval remains an authorised human authority workflow involving PHC and the relevant JAKIM Mufti, scholars and Halal officers; AI does not certify. Official connections are activated under the relevant authority’s authorization.

  1. AHTE
  2. Direct JAKIM API ⇅
  3. JAKIM authority system
  4. PHC + JAKIM Mufti / scholars / authorised Halal officers human review
boundaries

Shared confidence. Clear responsibilities.

PHC, GHSCL, AHTE, laboratories, Sinotrans, port/customs authorities, GCC authorities, financiers, Takaful operators and competent Halal authorities retain distinct responsibilities. Evidence, monitoring and cryptographic integrity do not create another party's decision.
