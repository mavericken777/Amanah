Build a preparation checklist before entering an authenticated application. Selections stay in this browser session; no company data is uploaded or stored.

Readiness selections are self-reported. They do not establish evidence completeness, eligibility or authority approval.

why

A dossier that stays useful after the audit

Readiness

Identify missing source, control, personnel and evidence records before an audit.

Continuity

Connect supplier and formula changes to the affected batch and control context.

Market preparation

Reconcile packaging, lot, pallet, container, seal and destination interfaces without promising approval.

Accountability

Give manufacturers, laboratories, auditors and authorities their own attributable responsibilities.

prepare

What to prepare

Company and facility

Legal entity, licenses, responsible persons, site ownership, layout, zones, equipment and controlled flows.

Products and materials

SKU, formulation/BOM and process versions; ingredients, raw materials, suppliers, origin and existing certificate references.

People and controls

Halal roles, competence, training, HCP/SCCP mapping, sanitation/segregation and change control.

Systems and evidence

ERP/MES/QMS/WMS/LIMS/IoT inventory, SOPs, monitoring logs, sampling, lab records and audit history.

journey

The manufacturer lifecycle

Enrollment is not certification. Final approval/disapproval is externally owned, and shipment enablement still requires applicable destination gates.

  1. Identity / due diligence
  2. Entity / facility qualification
  3. Product / formula / materials
  4. Suppliers / provenance
  5. Systems / digital twin
  6. Applicability / HCP / SCCP
  7. Evidence gaps / remediation / training
  8. Sample plan / pre-audit
  9. Smart-glass audit
  10. Findings / corrective action
  11. Re-verification
  12. Human authority workflow
  13. Approval / disapproval reference
  14. Monitoring / shipment readiness
