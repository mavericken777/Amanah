FOUR SYNCHRONIZED CHAINS · ARCHITECTURE

Follow the chain that answers your question.

source

Origin and production genealogy

Source → producer → supplier → material lot → sample → laboratory evidence → manufacturing batch → packaging lot. Each edge can carry evidence. A formula or supplier change identifies affected batches rather than erasing earlier lineage.

custody

Custody follows the physical object

Container and seal identity, actor, timestamp, condition and acceptance bind each handover. GPS, geofence, temperature, humidity, door and tamper signals are selected by commodity risk; every sensor is not mandatory for every product.

  2. Warehouse
  3. Booking / pickup
  4. Vehicle / container
  5. Seal / loading
  6. Custody transfer
  7. Port / export
  8. Transit
  9. GCC receiving

  11. Retail
binding

A physical event needs a digital binding

Facility ↔ FacilityTwin; material ↔ MaterialObject; batch ↔ BatchObject; sample ↔ SampleObject; container ↔ ContainerObject; seal ↔ SealObject; handover ↔ CustodyTransferEvent; recall ↔ RecallCase.

Technical detail

ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof. Source system, control, requirement, device and relationship references add the context necessary to assess the event.

disclosure

Traceability without unrestricted disclosure

Consumer / buyer

An issuer-authorized provenance, state and verification summary; no confidential formula or unrestricted supplier dossier.

Manufacturer / supplier

Own organization and scoped operational/evidence records.

Auditor / authority

Assigned evidence and regulatory scope with attributable access.

Importer / logistics / retail

Relevant shipment, custody, condition, receiving and destination records.
