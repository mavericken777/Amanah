Origin and genealogy

Every event has a digital binding

ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof, with source system, control, requirement, device and relationship references.

disclosure

Traceability without unrestricted disclosure

Role, organization, purpose and jurisdiction determine what manufacturers, auditors, authorities, logistics operators, importers, financiers and consumers can see. Cross-border default is minimum-necessary signed assertions, references, hashes and scoped metadata.
