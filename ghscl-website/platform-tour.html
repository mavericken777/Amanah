authority

Authority and decision boundary

AHTE

Standards, evidence, trust, intelligence, digital twins, event fabric, anomaly detection, predictive analytics and configured operational holds.

Direct JAKIM API

Target authority-connectivity interface for evidence/status exchange subject to official activation and authorization.

JAKIM / competent authority

Formal Halal certification and authority decisions remain with authorised human decision-makers.

AI boundary

AI may ingest, assess, recommend and support configured D4 holds. It does not independently certify Halal or execute D5/D6 sovereign/legal decisions.
