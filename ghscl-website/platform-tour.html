authority

Authority connectivity

AHTE

Standards, evidence, trust, intelligence, digital twins, event fabric, anomaly detection, predictive analytics and configured operational holds.

Direct JAKIM API

Target authority-connectivity interface for evidence/status exchange subject to official activation and authorization.

JAKIM / issuing organisation

Certification records and their scope are linked to the relevant organisation.

AI / ML support

AI/ML continuously monitors connected evidence, identifies anomalies, predicts risk and recommends preemptive strategies. Certification award and revocation are decided by JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors.

operating principles

Connected product records keep evidence and operating context together

Product continuity

The same product, SKU, batch and shipment context follows the journey from origin through destination operations.

Authority separation

AHTE trust state, authority state, operational state, customs state and finance state remain distinct.

Evidence integrity

Each material event is attributable to the relevant object, actor and time; cryptographic integrity supports review but does not replace truth assessment.

Exception handling

Detected issues propagate into containment, investigation, corrective action, re-verification and recall where required.
