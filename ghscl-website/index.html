12 / WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

An exception changes the next action.

Select a scenario to inspect the hold and review path.

Decision boundary and trust lifecycle

INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED requires the corresponding real evidence and accountable gates. Exceptions include HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED and RECALLED.

AI may perform D0–D2 and configured D4 holds. D3 recommendations support humans. AI cannot execute D5/D6, certify Halal, release sovereign holds, approve finance/Takaful or create legal title.
