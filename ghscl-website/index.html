12 / WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

An exception changes the next action.

Select a scenario to inspect the hold and review path.

Decision boundary and trust lifecycle

INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED requires the corresponding real evidence and accountable gates. Exceptions include HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED and RECALLED.

AI may perform D0–D2 and configured D4 holds. D3 recommendations support humans. AI cannot execute D5/D6, certify Halal, release sovereign holds, approve finance/Takaful or create legal title.

13 / GCC IMPORTER + DISTRIBUTOR + RETAIL

The destination is an operating chain, not a final dot.

GCC port / authority release reference→Importer receiving→Destination warehouse→Distributor / 3PL→Retailer / marketplace→Buyer / consumer verification

Importer workspace

Pre-arrival dossier, product/SKU/batch identity, credential status, container/seal reconciliation, quantity/condition checks, discrepancies, quarantine, inventory lots and distribution eligibility.

Retail / marketplace workspace

Listing eligibility, PO/ASN receiving, batch/lot traceability, storage, credential expiry, verification, withdrawal and recall propagation across stores, fulfilment centres and orders.

Importer, distributor and retailer states remain linked to the same product, batch, custody and authority references. The Command Center can trace recall blast radius through importer inventory, distributor transfers and affected retail locations/orders.
