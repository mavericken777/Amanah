SMART-GLASS SITE AUDIT

An observation becomes a traceable audit record.

Canonical project architecture. This page describes the required workflow; it does not claim an operational device integration or completed onboarding.

01

Authenticate and assign

Device identity, auditor identity, MFA and role/scope policy precede the assigned audit and facility verification.

02

Identify and capture

Load applicable requirements and HCPs, walk the process, scan the object and capture observations with image, video, document or sample references.

03

Assess and correct

AI may surface missing evidence, anomalies or contradictions with evidence and model references. The human auditor assesses findings and corrective actions.

04

Sign and reconcile

Preserve original media, hash evidence and sign the manifest where required. Encrypted offline events reconcile through sequence and signature checks; conflicts remain visible.
