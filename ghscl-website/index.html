WHAT AHTE IS

A platform layer, not a sovereign certification authority.

What it does

AHTE orchestrates source references, requirements, controls, HCP/SCCP, evidence, audit tests, findings, corrective action, re-verification, authority gates, trust state and operational release.

What it never does

AHTE does not create a halal certificate, replace a competent authority, turn a laboratory result into halal certification, or issue sovereign/legal determinations.
