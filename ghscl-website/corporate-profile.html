EVIDENCE

Evidence before trust.

Minimum binding: ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof. Evidence is append-only and provenance-linked. Hash proves integrity, not truth. Corrections occur by supersession.

LAB / AUDIT

Scientific evidence and accountable human review.

Sample → custody → method/QC → result → review/signature → evidence. NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL. AI assists with validation and analysis; audit observations, review notes, issuer details, and outcomes are captured in the product record.

DIRECT JAKIM API

A direct authority-connectivity architecture with explicit decision boundaries.

AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM. The integration layer is designed for authorized evidence/status exchange when official production specifications and credentials are available. Formal outcomes are shown with their issuing organisation.

LOGISTICS

China → GCC direct.

Sinotrans and other logistics participants contribute warehouse, pickup, pallet/lot, container/seal, telemetry, route, custody, port-transfer and proof-of-delivery evidence into the same end-to-end lineage. Malaysia is the governance/assurance/authority-connectivity plane unless separately scoped.

PORTS / CUSTOMS

Trust information supports border-system decisions; it does not replace them.

Authorized adapters can support minimum-necessary trust lookup and return inspection, sampling, hold and release events. Border-system control remains with the relevant authority.

COMMAND CENTER

Monitor, predict, intervene, verify.

The 24/7 operating model monitors evidence completeness, trust fractures, custody exceptions, predictive risk, CAPA, re-verification and recall blast radius. AHTE may support configured D4 holds; Review outcomes are recorded with their issuer and operating context.

GOVERNANCE

Evidence before trust. Trust before operational release.

Authority state, AHTE trust state, operational state, customs state and finance/Takaful state remain separate. AI supports evidence matching and risk analysis; the workflow records recommendations, reviews, and resulting status.
