Every object has a context.

Choose a node to inspect its designed relationships and evidence obligations. No production identifiers, evidence counts or timestamps are fabricated.

origin

Before the factory

Register source identity, material, lot, origin documents and applicable requirements before production. A sample remains bound to its material and collection history; a signed result without reconciled custody is insufficient.

  1. Source / producer identity
  2. Supplier and origin provenance
  3. Raw-material lot
  4. Sampling plan
  5. Collection and seal
  6. Chain of custody
  7. Lab accession and method
  8. Authenticated result
production

Within the operation

The facility twin connects zones, people, materials, processes and batches to the controls that require evidence. Supplier, formula or process changes trigger review of affected objects.

  1. Facility qualification
  2. Product / SKU / formula
  3. Applicability
  4. HCP / SCCP controls
  5. Evidence capture
  6. Smart-glass audit
  7. Finding / corrective action
  8. Re-verification
decision

Human authority and continuing monitoring

Authority decisions and system trust states are distinct. Approval conditions and validity remain attached to the decision scope. A later exception can require HOLD, re-verification or recall without rewriting the original evidence.

  1. Evidence dossier
  2. Authorized human review
  3. Approval / disapproval
  4. Authenticated authority reference
  5. Scoped trust view
  6. Continuing surveillance
market

From factory to market

Each physical handoff has identity, custody, condition and document checks. Destination acceptance and internal operational release remain separately evidenced gates.

  1. Packaging / lot / pallet
  2. Warehouse
  3. Pickup / container / seal
  4. Logistics / port / export
  5. Transit
  6. GCC arrival / import
  7. Receiving / distribution
  8. Retail / buyer verification
lab

Laboratory evidence is a native workflow

A laboratory connector carries accession, method scope, matrix, result, signatory, report version and correction history. Uploading a PDF alone does not establish authenticated laboratory evidence. NOT DETECTED ≠ HALAL.

  1. SampleID
  2. Collection
  3. Seal
  4. Custody
  5. Accession
  6. Method / QC
  7. Technical review
  8. Signed report
  9. Integrity validation
  10. AHTE evidence
standards

Requirements are resolved by scope

The standards architecture includes the project’s seventeen Malaysian Standards, alongside applicable MPPHM, MHMS, Meat & Poultry protocols, JAKIM circulars and destination instruments. Entity, product, process, activity, market and shipment determine applicability. Licensed clause text is not reproduced.
