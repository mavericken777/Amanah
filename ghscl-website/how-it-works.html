Build confidence before production begins

A material remains bound to source, lot, physical/digital identity, sample, custody and laboratory evidence before production decisions are made.

  1. Verified raw-material origin
  2. Supplier / producer identity
  3. Physical + digital identity
  4. Material lot
  5. Sample / seal
  6. Chain of custody
  7. China laboratory
  8. Signed scientific evidence

  6. Smart-glass audit
