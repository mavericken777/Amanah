AHTE / STANDARDS INTELLIGENCE

The complete applicable Malaysian/JAKIM Halal standards registry.

Amanah does not reduce Halal assurance to MS 1500 and MS 2400. AHTE evaluates the full current Malaysian/JAKIM standards registry together with every applicable product and technical instrument, then layers the certification framework, authority instruments, destination requirements and laboratory methods.

CERTIFICATION FRAMEWORK LAYER

Standards are applied inside the complete authority framework.

  1. MPPHM 2020
  2. MHMS 2020
  3. HAS
  4. IHCS
  5. protocols
  6. circulars
  7. authority instructions
  8. destination rules
  9. laboratory methods

AHTE resolves applicability by product, service, facility, process, logistics activity, destination and evidence context, then maps the applicable requirement to controls, HCP/SCCP obligations, evidence, audit tests, findings, CAPA and re-verification.
