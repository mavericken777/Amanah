mission purpose

What the China mission is demonstrating

The mission is not a disconnected technology roadshow. It demonstrates one operating model in which China-origin participants contribute attributable evidence, AHTE carries standards and trust context, Sinotrans preserves custody, and GCC actors receive, distribute, list and verify products while issuing organisations retain their decisions.

China origin

Manufacturers, suppliers, laboratories, auditors, traceability platforms, warehouses and logistics systems contribute evidence at the point of work.

Malaysia governance plane

PHC supports halal-industry ecosystem development, assurance coordination and institutional engagement; AHTE provides the standards/evidence/trust layer and direct JAKIM API target.

GCC destination

Importers, authorities/customs, receiving warehouses, distributors, retailers, marketplaces, institutional buyers and consumers act on destination-specific evidence and status.

24/7 Command Center

Cross-corridor events, exceptions, CAPA, custody, cybersecurity, receiving and recall are monitored without collapsing authority, operational, customs and finance states.

certification workflow

Continuous assurance and certification decisions

Amanah continuously monitors certified premises, products, laboratories, warehouses and logistics across the China → GCC journey. AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM connects the complete evidence and monitoring context. AI/ML provides real-time assessment, prediction and preemptive strategy support. JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors decide certification award and revocation.
