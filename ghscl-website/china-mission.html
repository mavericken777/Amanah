mission purpose

What the China mission is demonstrating

The mission is not a disconnected technology roadshow. It demonstrates one operating model in which China-origin participants contribute attributable evidence, AHTE carries standards and trust context, Sinotrans preserves custody, and GCC actors receive, distribute, list and verify products while competent authorities retain their decisions.

China origin

Manufacturers, suppliers, laboratories, auditors, traceability platforms, warehouses and logistics systems contribute evidence at the point of work.

Malaysia governance plane

PHC supports halal-industry ecosystem development, assurance coordination and institutional engagement; AHTE provides the standards/evidence/trust layer and direct JAKIM API target.

GCC destination

Importers, authorities/customs, receiving warehouses, distributors, retailers, marketplaces, institutional buyers and consumers act on destination-specific evidence and status.

24/7 Command Center

Cross-corridor events, exceptions, CAPA, custody, cybersecurity, receiving and recall are monitored without collapsing authority, operational, customs and finance states.

authority boundary

Authority and decision boundaries remain explicit

The mission demonstrates digital trust infrastructure without claiming that software, AI, blockchain, laboratories, smart glasses, QR/NFC, sensors or logistics partners create Halal certification. AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM is the controlling authority-connectivity topology; external production activation depends on authorised credentials and channels.

D0–D2

Ingest, encoded controls and machine assessment may be automated.

D3–D4

Recommendations and configured trust-fracture holds support accountable human operations.

D5–D6

Authority gates and sovereign/legal decisions remain with authorised humans / competent authority.

State separation

Authority state, AHTE trust state, operational state, customs/sovereign state and finance state remain separate.
