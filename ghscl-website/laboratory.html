Laboratory evidence is not certification

Scientific result

A result contributes analytical evidence to the assurance case.

Method and QC

Accredited scope, method controls and quality-control records determine how the result may be interpreted.

Authority decision

NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL. Certification remains with authorised humans and the competent authority.

LIMS and partner integration

Laboratory systems connect through controlled adapters and canonical evidence objects. Serialization or anti-counterfeit platforms may support identity and traceability, but they do not replace a complete LIMS scientific workflow.
