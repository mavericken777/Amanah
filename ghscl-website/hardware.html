02

Equipment is selected for its evidence role.

Smart glasses

RealWear Navigator 520 / Vuzix M400 reference classes

Hands-free observation, image/video and voice evidence. Bind auditor, checklist, location and signed audit session; AI assists and the auditor reviews.

Rugged handhelds

Zebra TC78 reference class

QR/NFC and item scanning at receiving, sampling and custody transfer. Authenticate the operator and bind each scan to its product or shipment.

RFID readers & printers

Zebra FX9600 / ZT610 RFID reference classes

Read and encode pallet or item identity. Reconcile the encoded identity with the physical object before it becomes a custody record.

Environmental sensing

Sensirion SHT85 / Vaisala HMP110 reference classes

Select the correct measurement range and calibrated probe for the use case. Frozen transport requires a validated low-temperature configuration.

Visual evidence

Axis M2036-LE reference class

Capture permitted inspection or custody imagery with time, actor and object context; apply access and retention controls.

Vehicle telemetry

Teltonika FMC650 reference class

Connect route, vehicle and operational events. Select compatible GNSS, temperature, door and refrigeration interfaces for the fleet.

Edge gateway

Moxa UC-8220 reference class

Buffer evidence locally, validate connectors and preserve sequence during network loss. Protect credentials and reconcile acknowledged receipt.

Industrial networking

Cisco Catalyst IE3400 Heavy Duty reference class

Segment device traffic, control access and support resilient connectivity between field equipment and secure ingestion.

04

Device lifecycle

Device trust continues after installation. Each change, service action and retirement remains tied to the equipment record.

01

Select & survey

Confirm operating temperature, installation, power, network, measurement accuracy, regional approvals and the evidence purpose.

02

Enrol & commission

Assign ownership, credentials and permitted interfaces. Verify scanning, clock alignment, secure ingestion and offline recovery before use.

03

Calibrate & verify

Record calibration method, interval, result and certificate. Expired calibration places affected measurements into review.

04

Monitor & maintain

Track health, battery, clock drift, sensor faults and connector status. Assign service actions and document their closure.

05

Update & re-verify

Authorise firmware or configuration changes, preserve change history and re-test the controls affected by the update.

06

Retire & replace

Revoke credentials, stop ingestion and preserve historical attribution. Commission the replacement with its own identity.
