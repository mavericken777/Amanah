Shariah finance & Takaful

Trust evidence can support finance without becoming the finance decision.

The target Shariah Financing API supports trade finance, Takaful evidence/cases and approved token/digital-value references subject to separate Shariah, legal and regulatory approval.

  2. Purpose-bound finance evidence packet
  3. Connector state
  4. Financier / Takaful / approved digital-value workflow
  5. External case / decision state
  6. Decision reference returned where authorised
services

Target services

Trade financing

Evidence support for Islamic trade finance, purchase-order and receivables finance.

Inventory/shipment financing

Asset, custody, shipment and exception-state evidence.

Takaful

Underwriting and claims evidence/case support; decisions remain external.

Tokenomics

External token/digital-asset references only where legal, regulatory and Shariah structures approve them.

boundaries

Hard boundaries

AHTE trust state is not a credit decision. Halal certification is not financing approval. AHTE is not the legal-title registry. Tokenization does not itself change ownership, legal title, regulatory status, Shariah status or certification state.
