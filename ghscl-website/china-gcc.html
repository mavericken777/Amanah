Connect warehousing and the complete logistics journey

Integration scope covers WMS/TMS/Y2T/MIS/EDI/IoT, booking, pickup, warehouse receipt/dispatch, segregation/storage, pallet/lot, container/seal, telemetry, route/geofence, custody, port transfer and proof of delivery.

The interface supports minimum-necessary shipment trust resolution and sovereign inspection/sampling/hold/release event return. AHTE does not override port/customs authority.
