route

Default physical route

Do not describe the default Shipment 001 route as China → Malaysia → GCC.

  1. China origin
  2. Traceability / laboratory / manufacturer
  3. Sinotrans warehouse
  4. Origin port/customs
  5. International transit
  6. GCC port/customs
  7. Importer / receiving
  8. Warehouse / distribution / retail
sinotrans

Sinotrans is warehouse + end-to-end logistics

The target integration covers WMS/TMS/Y2T/MIS/EDI/IoT, booking, pickup, warehouse receipt/dispatch, segregation/storage, pallet/lot, container/seal, telemetry, route/geofence, custody, port transfer and proof of delivery.

ports

Port/customs authorities receive an API/trust interface

The interface supports minimum-necessary shipment trust resolution and sovereign inspection/sampling/hold/release event return. AHTE does not override port/customs authority.

pilot

Shipment 001 remains a pilot until real evidence exists

Architecture and simulations do not instantiate a shipment. Real entity, product, buyer/importer, batch, authority, lab, logistics and destination evidence are required.
