Verify a product, batch or shipment disclosure

Product, batch and shipment IDs require an issuer-authorized disclosure token. This form cannot search private factory records. The token is sent only to the existing AHTE verification service.

views

A view that matches the verifier

Buyer / consumer

An authorized disclosure may summarize product, batch, origin, trust state, authority reference, destination and last update. Unissued fields remain undisclosed.

Authority / auditor

A deeper view requires authenticated role and assigned scope; public verification does not grant authority access.

Product / batch / shipment

Identifiers resolve through authorized records and token scope. No public identifier directory or unrestricted evidence search is introduced.

Integrity and status

The service returns the packet’s recorded content hash and state. Retrieval alone does not independently verify an issuer signature or certify the product.

meaning

What the result means

A successful response confirms that a current, non-revoked disclosure token resolved to its bound packet. System trust state, formal certification and operational release remain distinct. Missing or expired tokens do not prove that a product is non-halal.
