## Identity

The hexagonal infrastructure monogram (`media/ghscl-monogram.svg`) is an original code-drawn brand symbol. It is not an official authority seal. The angular GHSCL wordmark (`media/ghscl-wordmark.svg`) uses vector paths for the five initials. Do not substitute it for certification marks.

Palette: obsidian #08090B; platinum #C3C5C8; warm ivory #F0EDE5; architectural gold #B39A69. Use gold for chapter position, focal accents and primary actions. Avoid luminous green and neon. Contrast and legibility take priority over muted aesthetic.

Typography: Inter Tight for large editorial headlines; Manrope for body copy; DM Mono for indices and provenance labels. System fallbacks remain usable when remote fonts are unavailable. Headlines use restrained weight, tight tracking and one clear idea. Paragraph measure stays under 70 characters where possible.

Composition: 6vw desktop margins, a 1360px content maximum, 120px section rhythm, 20–24px mobile margins and 80px mobile rhythm. Corporate mark clear space: one-quarter mark width. Never distort vector assets. A gold ring or hexagon is decorative brand language, never an authority badge.

Motion: slow globe rotation, pointer response, ordered chapter changes, restrained media parallax and a timed opening identity cue. Muted by default. Sound starts only through user interaction. Reduced motion presents all eight chapters as readable static content; unavailable WebGL falls back to architectural imagery. Hero rendering pauses offscreen and when hidden; resolution capped at 1.5 device pixels.

## Assets and honest provenance

Four original AI-generated concept renders were produced with the built-in imagegen tool, visually inspected and converted to WebP for delivery. They depict imagined architecture, a port, a physical trust-packet metaphor and a control room. They are not photographs of actual operations, current shipments, laboratories or government facilities. Full generation prompts are in `media/production-prompts.json`.

A new 24-second concept motion study is assembled from the four AI renders with restrained camera pushes and fades. The master is 1280×720; web delivery is 960×540 at 24fps as H.264 MP4 and VP9 WebM (`ghscl-hybrid-film.*`), each under 650KB. Frames are AI-generated still CGI; movement is editorial pan/zoom, not animated 3D scene geometry. The original `ghscl-trust-film.mp4` is retained as a historical procedural network study. The new film supports scroll scrubbing and stakeholder playback controls. No commissioned photography or photoreal animated 3D product film is claimed. `scripts/render-ghscl-film.py` reproduces the delivery film from the stored renders with FFmpeg or the imageio-ffmpeg package.
