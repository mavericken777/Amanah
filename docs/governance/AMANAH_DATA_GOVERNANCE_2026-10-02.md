# AMANAH Data Governance
**Status:** PROJECT-DEFINED / CONTROLLED

Data is classified as public, internal, confidential, restricted-authority, or restricted-commercial. Canonical records retain owner, creator, jurisdiction, confidentiality, retention, version, timestamps, evidence references and audit history.

## Rules
- Evidence is append-only; corrections use supersession links.
- Authority decision records are distinct from AHTE trust, operational, customs and finance states.
- Purpose/role/tenant constraints govern access; exports are logged.
- Cross-border transfers require a registered legal basis and destination control before production activation.
- Retention/deletion rules must not destroy legally required audit evidence.
- Source bindings preserve provenance and version.
