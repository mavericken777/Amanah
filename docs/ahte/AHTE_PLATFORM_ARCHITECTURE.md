# AHTE Platform Architecture

AHTE is the continuous assurance and monitoring platform layer in Amanah. It connects applicable requirements, controls, evidence, certification workflow, product/SKU, premises, laboratory, audit, production, custody, warehouse, logistics, ports and GCC destination events.

**Topology:** `AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM`

**Corridor:** `China → GCC direct`

PHC and JAKIM work in parallel across Perak/state and federal governance. JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors decide certification award or revocation. AHTE supports their work with source-linked evidence, real-time monitoring, AI/ML predictive analytics, impact analysis and preemptive strategy recommendations, and records and propagates their outcomes across affected product and premises records.

The connected flow covers manufacturer onboarding; facility and product identity; suppliers and materials; standards applicability; lab sample custody, method, QC, result, review and signature; smart audit; findings and CAPA; production and digital twins; warehouse and Sinotrans custody; ports/customs; GCC importer, receiving, distribution, retail and verification; and continuous Command Center monitoring and recall support.
