# Platform delivery map

Amanah and AHTE connect the operating journey from manufacturer onboarding through product and premises assurance, laboratory evidence, smart audit, production, warehouse and logistics monitoring, ports and customs interfaces, GCC receiving, distribution, consumer verification and continuous recall readiness.

## Decision and monitoring model

PHC and JAKIM operate in parallel across Perak/state and federal governance. JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors decide certification award and revocation. AI/ML assists with real-time monitoring, evidence assessment, predictive analytics and preemptive strategies.

**Authority connectivity:** AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM
**Physical corridor:** China → GCC direct

## Product areas

- Manufacturer onboarding, organisation identity, premises, product/SKU, suppliers and applicable standards.
- Laboratory sample custody, methods, quality controls, results, review and signed evidence.
- Smart audit, findings, corrective action, re-verification and certification decision records.
- Production, digital-twin events, warehouse handling, Sinotrans custody and telemetry.
- Port/customs interfaces, GCC importer, destination warehouse, distributor, retailer and verification.
- 24/7 Command Center with live monitoring, risk prediction, preemptive strategy and recall impact analysis.
- Shariah finance and Takaful evidence interfaces for participating providers.

## Engineering practice

Maintain tenant isolation, least-privilege access, row-level security, protected documents, attributable audit events, safe connector states and repeatable test/build checks. Release evidence records the source revision, CI results and observed deployment revision.
