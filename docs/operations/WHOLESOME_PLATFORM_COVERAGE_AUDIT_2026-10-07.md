Those gaps are now explicitly closed in the master prompt, public standards experience, protected application routes, destination playbook, Command Center narrative and this controlling audit.

The remaining execution risk is no longer architecture coverage. It is **deliverable conversion quality**: turning complete content into finished boardroom-grade decks, DOCX/PDF packs, MOAs, partner binders, visuals and film assets.

## Standards completeness determination

The frozen source package defines a current controlled primary catalogue of 17 standards and explicitly requires all additional verified applicable product/technical instruments to enter the registry by scope.

Therefore the correct platform statement is:

> AMANAH/AHTE uses the complete controlled Malaysian/JAKIM standards registry and the complete applicable wider framework. It is not limited to MS 1500 and MS 2400 and is not conceptually capped to 17 standards.

Do not replace that rule with an invented list of standards not present in the verified source package.

## Importer / retailer determination

Importer and retailer are not optional downstream labels. They are operational actors with their own:

- onboarding;
- data objects;
- workflow;
- evidence;
- decisions;
- exceptions;
- Command Center views;
- KPIs;
- recall responsibilities;
- integration requirements;
- public and authenticated platform experiences.

The required destination chain is:

**GCC Port / Customs → GCC Importer → Receiving / Quarantine / Warehouse → Distributor / 3PL → Retailer / Marketplace / E-commerce → Buyer / Authority / Retailer / Consumer Verification → Recall / Post-Market Response**
