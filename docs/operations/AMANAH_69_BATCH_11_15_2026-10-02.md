## Item 11 — document and evidence management

Minimum evidence object:
`EvidenceID + organization/project/control/object binding + evidence class/type + source URI + content hash + collected/validity timestamps + status + metadata + actor + created timestamp`.

Controls:
- evidence is never treated as certification merely because it is hashed;
- release gates require verified, current, object-bound evidence;
- cross-organization evidence references are rejected by database enforcement;
- historical audit evidence is retained rather than silently overwritten;
- corrections use new/superseding records or metadata lineage rather than erasing the original record;
- confidential records remain organization/RLS scoped.

## Item 12 — AI-assisted review

AI may perform D0 ingest, D1 encoded-control processing, D2 machine assessment and configured D4 holds. D3 is recommendation. D5 authority gate and D6 sovereign/legal decisions are reserved.

Required AI provenance:
- assessment/case identifier;
- source/evidence references;
- model/provider/version reference when AI is used;
- output/rationale/confidence where available;
- escalation/hold state;
- human reviewer/final decision reference.

No AI output may be promoted into certification, sovereign release, financing/Takaful approval or legal title.

## Item 13 — human governance

Decision rights are separated by role and mandate:
- initiator/submitter;
- reviewer/auditor;
- operational approver where authorized;
- authority decision-maker for D5;
- sovereign/legal decision-maker for D6;
- administrator as platform operator, not substitute authority.

Signed/final external authority decisions require external reference and signature hash. Machine HITM execution cannot perform D5/D6.
