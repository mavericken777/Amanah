# Current Amanah platform status

Updated: 9 October 2026

Amanah and AHTE continuously connect and monitor the complete halal assurance journey for certified premises and products, including SKUs, suppliers, laboratories, audits, production, warehouses, logistics, ports, GCC receiving, distribution and verification. The Command Center connects certification status, evidence, custody, exceptions, risk, predictive analytics and preemptive strategy recommendations.

## Governance and decision workflow

PHC and JAKIM work in parallel across Perak/state and federal governance within Malaysia’s shared Islamic framework. JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors decide certification award and revocation through their applicable process. AI/ML assists with evidence review, real-time monitoring, risk prediction and recommendations.

System topology: `AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM`

Physical corridor: `China → GCC direct`

## Current implementation areas

- Public website: interactive twenty-stage process journey, responsive black-and-gold system, neutral Arial-family typography, shield-free home page and ecosystem service pages.
- Application: manufacturer onboarding, evidence, laboratory, audit/CAPA, production monitoring, custody, destination operations, Command Center, analytics and integration interfaces.
- Project reference: current applicable Malaysian/JAKIM standards and destination instruments in the maintained source register.
- Private trip administration, agreement drafts, signatories, personal identifiers and transaction-specific records remain outside the public repositories.
