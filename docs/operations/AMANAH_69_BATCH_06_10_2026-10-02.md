## 8. Facility digital twin and readiness

Each facility twin binds:

`Manufacturer → Facility → ProductionLine → Zones/Processes → Products/SKUs → Controls/HCP/SCCP → Devices/Sensors → Evidence → Audit → Findings/CAPA → Readiness`

### Readiness dimensions

- legal/site identity;
- declared operating scope;
- production lines and process boundaries;
- product/SKU allocation;
- material ingress/storage/process/egress;
- applicable controls and evidence;
- hygiene/segregation/contamination-control evidence where applicable;
- device/telemetry inventory;
- laboratory/sample plan;
- audit status and open findings;
- CAPA/re-verification;
- credential/authority references;
- connector state.

Readiness is an AHTE operational/assurance assessment. It is not certification.

## 10. Supplier, ingredient and raw-material provenance graph

Canonical provenance chain:

`Product/SKU → FormulaMaterial → Ingredient/RawMaterial → Supplier → MaterialLot → Origin → Certificate/Evidence → Receiving/Custody → Consumption/Batch`

### Required provenance controls

1. Supplier identity and facility/site reference where applicable.
2. Material identity and role (ingredient/raw material/other controlled material).
3. Lot/batch and origin references.
4. Evidence/certificate references with issuer, scope, validity and provenance.
5. Receiving and custody events.
6. Formula/BOM linkage and affected product/SKU.
7. Change/expiry/suspension events.
8. Blast-radius traversal from material/supplier/lot to affected products/batches/shipments.
9. Evidence integrity and supersession.
10. No inference that a supplier certificate automatically certifies downstream product.

### Trust-fracture handling

Missing, expired, contradictory or superseded material evidence routes to evidence-gap/exception logic and may create a configured D4 hold. AI may detect and recommend; authorized humans resolve material outcomes. D5/D6 remain authority-owned.
