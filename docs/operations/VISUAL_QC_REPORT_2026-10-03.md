## Public visual-claim rules

Do not display as factual without source evidence:
- “100% compliant”;
- “JAKIM connected/live”;
- “certified by blockchain/AI/lab”;
- fake certification numbers;
- fabricated shipment maps/telemetry presented as live;
- invented government/partner seals or endorsements;
- unverified offices, client counts, transaction volumes, uptime or vulnerability claims.
