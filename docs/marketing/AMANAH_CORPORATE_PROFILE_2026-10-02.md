## 1. Institutional identity

**Global Halal Supply Chain Limited (GHSCL Hong Kong)** is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle for the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem. It coordinates China-origin participation, China → GCC direct corridor execution, integration workstreams and 24/7 Command Center operations.

**AMANAH** is the operational workspace through which participating organizations manage identity, facilities, products, suppliers, evidence, laboratory records, audits, corrective action, monitoring, custody, logistics, authority-connected workflows and verification.

**AHTE** is the standards, evidence, trust and assurance intelligence layer. It connects applicability, controls, HCP/SCCP, evidence, digital twins, event fabric, cryptographic integrity, AI/ML analysis, exception management, CAPA, re-verification and recall/blast-radius workflows.

**PHC** is the Perak State Government halal-industry GLC represented in the project architecture for governance, industry development and authority connectivity.

## 2. Mission

Create a durable digital trust infrastructure for Halal and Tayyib trade in which evidence travels with the physical object, responsibilities remain explicit, technology assists accountable people, and operational release occurs only after required trust and authority gates.

## 5. Authority and decision boundary

AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM is the controlling authority-connectivity topology.

AI may ingest, encode controls, assess evidence and support configured D4 trust-fracture holds. AI does not execute D5 authority gates or D6 sovereign/legal decisions. AI, blockchain, QR/NFC, sensors, laboratories, tokens and platform trust states do not independently create Halal certification.

Formal Halal certification and sovereign/legal decisions remain with authorised human decision-makers and competent authorities. Port/customs release remains sovereign. Finance and Takaful decisions remain with approved providers.

## 6. Trust model

Minimum binding:

`ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof`

Evidence is append-only, provenance-linked, hash/signature-aware and corrected by supersession. Hash proves integrity of recorded content; it does not establish the truth of the underlying claim.

Trust lifecycle:

`INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED`

Exception states include HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED and RECALLED.

Authority state, AHTE trust state, operational state, customs state and finance state remain separate.

## 7. China → GCC operating corridor

The default physical corridor is **China → GCC direct**. Malaysia is the governance, assurance and authority-connectivity plane unless a separate physical movement is explicitly scoped.

China-side participation can include producers, suppliers, manufacturers, ERP/MES/QMS/WMS/LIMS/IoT/DMS systems, traceability, laboratories, smart-audit tools, Sinotrans warehouse/logistics and origin-port interfaces.

GCC-side participation can include importer, port/customs, receiving warehouse, distribution, retail and verification.

`[PILOT: Shipment 001 — China → GCC direct]` remains NOT-INSTANTIATED until real product, buyer/importer, purchase/cargo evidence, laboratory evidence, authority records, custody events and destination evidence exist.

The GHSCL Command Center operates as the cross-corridor coordination plane for:

- evidence integrity and expiry;
- product/facility/batch exceptions;
- laboratory and audit status;
- Sinotrans custody and telemetry;
- origin/destination port events;
- predictive risk and preemptive strategy;
- CAPA and re-verification;
- recall and blast-radius analysis;

The Command Center monitors and coordinates; it does not substitute for sovereign authority decisions.
