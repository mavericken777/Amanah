## Executive profile

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle within the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem. Its role is to coordinate the China-to-GCC operating corridor, connect participating manufacturers, laboratories, logistics operators, ports/customs, importers and downstream stakeholders, and operate the 24/7 Command Center together with the applicable governance and authority-connectivity model.

GHSCL does not create Halal certification. The platform organizes evidence, controls, custody, audit, risk and trust state so authorized humans and competent authorities can make accountable decisions.

## Evidence model

Minimum binding:

`ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof`

Evidence is append-only and provenance-linked. Corrections occur by supersession. A hash proves integrity of recorded content, not the truth of the claim.

## Trust lifecycle

Normal:
INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED

Exception states:
HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED, RECALLED.

Authority state, AHTE trust state, operational state, customs state and finance/Takaful state remain separate.

## China-origin ecosystem

Manufacturers and suppliers retain their ERP/MES/QMS/WMS/LIMS/IoT/DMS systems. AHTE connects evidence rather than requiring wholesale replacement. Physical/digital product identity, anti-counterfeit functions and smart-glass audit may be integrated where scoped.

## Laboratory

Sample → custody → method/QC → result → review/signature → evidence.

**NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL.**

Laboratory results support evidence. They do not create certification.

## Ports and customs

Authorized trust-resolution and event interfaces can exchange minimum-necessary evidence and status. Sovereign inspection, hold and release remain with the relevant authority.

## GCC destination

Importer and receiving controls connect destination acceptance, warehouse, distribution, retail and verification to the same evidence lineage.

## Command Center

The 24/7 GHSCL + JAKIM Command Center operating model monitors evidence integrity, exceptions, custody, predictive risk, corrective action, re-verification and recall exposure.

AI may ingest, encode controls, assess and support configured trust-fracture holds. AI may not execute D5/D6 sovereign/legal decisions.

## Finance and Takaful

AHTE may prepare purpose-bound evidence packets and case-state integration for approved Islamic-finance and Takaful providers. Financing and underwriting decisions remain with those providers. Tokenization, where used, requires separate legal, Shariah and regulatory approval.

## Public verification

Issuer-authorized disclosures may support buyers, receiving teams, distributors and retail verification without exposing unrelated confidential records.
