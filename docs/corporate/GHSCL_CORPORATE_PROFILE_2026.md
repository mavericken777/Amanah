## 1. Executive profile

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited (GHSCL Hong Kong) is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle within the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem.

GHSCL coordinates the China-to-GCC operating corridor and connects manufacturers, suppliers, laboratories, logistics operators, ports/customs, importers, distribution and retail through the AMANAH platform and the AHTE trust and assurance engine.

GHSCL is designed as an operating layer for evidence continuity, cross-border assurance, stakeholder integration and 24/7 exception response. It does not create Halal certification. Technology organizes evidence, controls, custody, audit and trust state so authorized humans and competent authorities can make accountable decisions.

## 9. Laboratory, smart audit and human governance

### Laboratory evidence chain

**Sample → custody → method/QC → result → technical review/signature → evidence**

**NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL.**

Laboratory results support evidence. They do not create Halal certification.

### Smart audit

Assigned audit → facility/scope verification → applicable requirements / HCPs → physical walkthrough → object identification → evidence capture → local hash → AI assist → human assessment → finding / CAR → CAPA → re-verification → sign → sync / reconcile → authority workflow.

### Human governance

The platform preserves initiator, reviewer, approver, rejector, override, dual-approval, evidence, timestamp and immutable audit trail.

AI assists. Authorized humans decide.
