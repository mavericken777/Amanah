## 1. Executive positioning

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle within the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem. GHSCL coordinates the operating environment that connects manufacturers, suppliers, laboratories, audit teams, logistics operators, ports/customs, GCC importers, downstream distribution and authorized verification.

The operating objective is to make cross-border Halal assurance **inspectable, attributable and operational** without collapsing the distinct responsibilities of competent authorities, commercial operators, laboratories, logistics providers, technology platforms or human decision-makers.

GHSCL does **not** create Halal certification. AMANAH and AHTE organize evidence, controls, audit, custody, trust state, predictive risk and decision workflow so authorized humans and competent authorities can make accountable decisions.

**Operating principle:** **Evidence before trust. Trust before operational release.**

## 7. Evidence and trust architecture

Minimum binding:

**ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof**

Evidence is:
- append-only;
- provenance-linked;
- hash/signature-aware;
- corrected through supersession;
- attributable to its originating actor/system;
- linked to exact objects and events.

A hash proves integrity of recorded content. It does **not** prove the truth of the underlying claim.

Normal trust lifecycle:

**INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED**

Exception states:

**HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED, RECALLED**

Authority state, AHTE trust state, operational state, customs state and finance/Takaful state remain separate.

## 8. AI and human governance

Decision classes:
- **D0** — ingest;
- **D1** — encoded control;
- **D2** — machine assessment;
- **D3** — recommendation;
- **D4** — trust-fracture hold;
- **D5** — authority gate;
- **D6** — sovereign/legal decision.

AI may perform D0-D2 and configured D4 holds. D3 is recommendation. D5/D6 remain authorized human / competent-authority / sovereign decisions.

AI may not:
- certify Halal;
- release sovereign holds;
- approve financing or Takaful;
- create legal title;
- silently replace an authorized human decision.

Machine outputs remain attributable to model/version, prompt-policy, evidence source, output, confidence, reviewer and final human decision.
