# AMANAH Global Halal Digital Trust Academy

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Control date:** 2026-10-03  
**Status:** PROJECT-DEFINED TRAINING ACADEMY / NORMATIVE CONTENT SOURCE-LOCKED

## 2. Core curriculum

### Module 1 — Architecture and authority
AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM; China → GCC direct; authority/AHTE/operational/customs/finance state separation.

### Module 2 — Standards and source fidelity
How applicability and source bindings work. Exact normative wording is taught only from licensed/verified source material. Where unavailable: DATA NOT AVAILABLE — SOURCE-LOCKED.

### Module 3 — Evidence
ObjectID, EventID, EvidenceID, ActorID, Timestamp, IntegrityProof; append-only provenance; signatures/hashes; supersession.

### Module 4 — Manufacturer / facility / product
Organisation, KYC evidence, facility twin, product/SKU, formula/BOM, suppliers, ingredients/raw materials and change triggers.

### Module 5 — Laboratory
Sample → custody → method/QC → result → review/signature → evidence. NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL.

### Module 6 — Audit / CAPA
Audit planning, object identification, evidence capture, finding, corrective action, re-verification and signed session.

### Module 7 — Production and monitoring
Batch/lot identity, sensors, equipment, exception detection, trust-fracture HOLD and human disposition.

### Module 8 — Logistics / ports / GCC
Warehouse, container, seal, telemetry, custody, ports/customs, importer receiving and downstream verification.

### Module 9 — AI governance
D0–D6; AI may support D0–D2 and configured D4; D5/D6 reserved.

### Module 10 — Command Center
Alert severity, assignment, escalation, predictive risk, preemptive strategy, recall/blast radius and evidence preservation.

## 4. Assessment

Project-defined competence model:
- knowledge assessment;
- practical workflow assessment;
- critical-boundary assessment;
- trainer/reviewer sign-off.

A participant fails the critical-boundary component if they assert any of the following:
- AI can certify Halal;
- laboratory result alone creates Halal status;
- blockchain/hash proves factual truth;
- AHTE can release a sovereign customs hold;
- a simulated event is production evidence.

Numeric pass marks may be configured by programme owner; where a statutory qualification has an authority-defined mark, that authority source controls.

## 5. Competence record

Minimum:
- PersonID;
- OrganisationID;
- role;
- module/version;
- trainer;
- delivery date;
- assessment result;
- evidence reference;
- validity/refresh date;
- superseded record reference;
- signature/approval where required.

## 6. Change-trigger training

Retraining may be triggered by:
- material standards/instrument update;
- process/product/facility change;
- new system/interface;
- repeated finding;
- security incident;
- role change;
- new jurisdiction/destination;
- audit/authority instruction;
- major platform release.

## 7. Training environment

Training uses sandbox/development data. It must not create production shipments, authority decisions, laboratory results, finance/Takaful decisions or legal title.
