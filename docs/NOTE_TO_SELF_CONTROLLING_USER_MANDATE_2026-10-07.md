## 3. Authority boundary

AI, blockchain, QR/NFC, sensors, laboratories, tokens and software do not create Halal certification.

**AI assists. Authorised humans and competent authorities decide.**

Decision classes:

- D0 — ingest
- D1 — encoded control
- D2 — machine assessment
- D3 — recommendation
- D4 — configured trust-fracture hold
- D5 — authority gate
- D6 — sovereign / legal decision

AI may perform D0-D2 and configured D4 holds. D5/D6 remain with authorised humans / competent authority.

## 8. Trust model

Minimum evidence binding:

**ObjectID + EventID + EvidenceID + ActorID + Timestamp + IntegrityProof**

Evidence is append-only, provenance-linked, hash/signature-aware and corrected by supersession.

**Hash proves integrity, not truth.**

Trust lifecycle:

**INITIAL → EVIDENCE-COMPLETE → ASSESSED → VERIFIED → RELEASED**

Exception states include HOLD, QUARANTINED, DISPUTED, CORRECTIVE-ACTION, RE-VERIFICATION, EXPIRED, SUSPENDED, REVOKED, RECALLED.

Keep authority, AHTE trust, operational, customs and finance states separate.
