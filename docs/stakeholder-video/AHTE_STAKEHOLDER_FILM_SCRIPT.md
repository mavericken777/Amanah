# AHTE Stakeholder Explainer Film — Master Script

[PROPOSAL: communications artifact derived from canonical AHTE architecture — not authority text]

## Audience
Government stakeholders, competent authorities, certification bodies, laboratories, manufacturers, logistics providers, importers/buyers, technology partners and strategic investors.

## Target duration
Approximately 4-5 minutes.

## Editorial rule
The film must never say or imply that AHTE certifies halal, replaces JAKIM/JAIN/MAIN or GCC destination authorities, or turns laboratory/AI results into sovereign certification.

## Sequence

### 00:00-00:20 — Opening
Visual: Global supply chain network, product/batch/container identity moving from origin to destination.
Narration: "Modern halal trade needs more than a certificate image. It needs a trustworthy chain of evidence from source, through control and verification, to the physical movement of goods."

### 00:20-00:45 — The problem
Visual: fragmented documents, spreadsheets, laboratory reports, certificates, logistics events and disconnected systems.
Narration: "Today, critical evidence can sit across manufacturers, laboratories, certifiers, logistics operators, ports and importers. The challenge is not simply collecting data. It is preserving source, context, integrity, accountability and decision rights."

### 00:45-01:15 — What AHTE is
Visual: architecture layer between authority, standards, industry systems and physical supply chain.
Narration: "The Amanah Halal Trust Ecosystem, operated by Global Halal Supply Chain Ltd HK, is designed as an orchestration and evidence layer. It connects standards, requirements, controls, evidence, audit, human decisions, trust state and operational release."

### 01:15-01:50 — Canonical control path
Visual: animated flow.
Narration: "The operating path is explicit: Authority, Standard or Instrument, Requirement, Applicability, Control, Halal Control Point or Supplier Control Point, Evidence, Audit Test, Finding, Corrective Action, Re-verification, Authority Gate, Trust State and Operational Release."

### 01:50-02:20 — AI + HITM
Visual: AI model proposes assessment; a human decision panel remains visually dominant.
Narration: "AI can ingest, correlate, assess and recommend. It cannot remove a reserved authority gate. Human-in-the-mandate decisions remain accountable. D5 and D6 authority and sovereign decisions are reserved."

### 02:20-02:45 — Evidence and laboratory
Visual: sample collection, chain of custody, laboratory instrument, report hash, evidence object.
Narration: "Laboratory evidence is captured with provenance, method, sample and integrity metadata. An analytical result is evidence. It is not, by itself, a halal certification decision."

### 02:45-03:15 — Digital Audit Twin
Visual: physical factory, batch, pallet and container mirrored by a digital twin.
Narration: "The Digital Audit Twin links the physical world to a controlled digital evidence graph: facility, process, material, product, batch, custody, documents, tests, findings and decisions."

### 03:15-03:45 — Platinum monitoring
Visual: sensors, telemetry, geofence, seal, event stream and alert.
Narration: "At the Platinum layer, device identity and telemetry can contribute continuous evidence: temperature, humidity, seal status, location, custody and exception events. A trust fracture can place an entity on hold rather than silently allowing release."

### 03:45-04:15 — China → GCC pilot
Visual: China origin → factory → laboratory → container → port → GCC destination → receiving.
Narration: "The first controlled transaction is the China to GCC direct corridor pilot. Shipment 001 is not a fictional certification. It is a transaction model whose real closure depends on real SKU, manufacturer, importer, documents, evidence, custody and destination release."

### 04:15-04:40 — Trust state and release
Visual: hard gates first, trust vector second, release last.
Narration: "A hard-gate failure cannot be offset by a high score. Trust scoring is descriptive after eligibility. Operational release is a controlled operational state, not a certification."

### 04:40-05:00 — Closing
Visual: clean stakeholder dashboard, authority boundary labels, secure data flow.
Narration: "AHTE is built to make evidence easier to trust, decisions easier to audit, and physical trade easier to monitor—while preserving the authority of the people and institutions empowered to decide."

## On-screen phrases
- "Evidence-led. Source-aware. Authority-bounded."
- "AI assists. Human authority decides."
- "Hard gates are non-compensable."
- "NOT DETECTED != HALAL"
- "Operational release != certification"
- "[PILOT: Shipment 001 — China → GCC direct]"
