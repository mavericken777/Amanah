# CODA / China Enterprise Digital-Trust Enablement — Hardware Financing Executive Pack

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Control date:** 2026-10-02 | **Status:** COMMERCIAL PROPOSAL — COUNTERPARTY VALIDATION REQUIRED

## Purpose

Provide a structured 3 / 5 / 7-year financing framework for manufacturer digitalisation and assurance hardware used in the China → GCC direct corridor. This pack does not assert that CODA, a bank, a fund or a government programme has approved funding.

## Eligible reference scope

Platinum reference hardware may include rugged handhelds/tablets, smart glasses, RFID readers/printers, environmental sensors, industrial cameras, vehicle telematics, industrial gateways/networking and installation/accessory kits. Exact manufacturer/model/part number is controlled by `docs/hardware/PLATINUM_HARDWARE_MASTER_BOM_2026-10-02.json`.

## Financing model

For financed equipment principal **P**, annual nominal financing rate **r**, and monthly term **n**, indicative equal monthly payment:

`Payment = P × i / (1 - (1+i)^(-n))`, where `i = r/12`.

This is a scenario calculator only. Shariah structure, pricing method, taxes, fees, FX, security, residual value, insurance/Takaful and legal documentation must be agreed by the actual financier.

## Proposed commercial architecture

1. Manufacturer completes Amanah readiness and facility/device BOM.
2. GHSCL/AHTE creates a purpose-bound hardware/evidence package.
3. CODA may support enterprise mobilisation/introduction under its agreed mandate.
4. Approved financier independently performs credit, KYC, Shariah, legal and security review.
5. Vendor/RFQ pricing is validated.
6. Financing contract is executed directly by the authorized parties.
7. Equipment is supplied, commissioned and bound to DeviceID / facility / control context.
8. Payment/service lifecycle is monitored separately from Halal certification state.

## Commercial protections

- No financing approval is created by AHTE.
- No CODA funding commitment is claimed.
- Hardware title/security interests remain governed by executed financing documents.
- Takaful/insurance is externally underwritten.
- Prices in the master BOM are planning references until RFQ.
- Any subsidy/grant/support programme must be evidenced from the administering body before being represented as available.
