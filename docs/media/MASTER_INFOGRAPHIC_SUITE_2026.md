## Visual identity

Obsidian / charcoal field, restrained metallic gold, warm ivory, Arial / Helvetica / neutral sans-serif, fine Islamic geometric rhythm and information-bearing linework.

No oversized decorative shield, generic AI icon cloud, cartoon graphic or meaningless ornament. Every plate must communicate a real authority, evidence, standards, custody, operating, risk, finance or verification relationship.
