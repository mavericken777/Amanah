# China Mission — Meeting Brief Book

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Control date:** 2026-10-03  
**Use:** executive meeting preparation / outcome capture  
**Rule:** a meeting brief frames questions; it does not prove a relationship, commitment or authority.

## Standard 1-page meeting structure

**Counterparty:** exact legal entity to confirm  
**Participants:** names/titles/decision rights  
**Meeting objective:** one sentence  
**Decision sought:** explicit and measurable  
**Evidence requested:** documents / system demonstration / technical owner  
**Commercial question:** fees, lead time, contracting path — classify as proposal until validated  
**Technical question:** endpoint/protocol/auth/data owner/sandbox/UAT  
**Authority boundary:** no technology/provider independently creates Halal certification  
**Close:** actions, owners, dates, evidence references.

## Brief 01 — CODA / industrial network

### Objective
Secure a controlled manufacturer/industrial introduction pipeline and agree the mechanism for moving candidates into AMANAH onboarding.

### Ask
- Which exact legal CODA entity/team is participating?
- Which industrial parks/sectors can be introduced?
- Who owns each introduction?
- Can candidates provide legal entity, facility, product, system and existing certification/evidence records?
- What commercial arrangement is proposed, and by whom?

### Required output
Named candidate list, introduction owners, target dates, sector, facility location, product category, contact, and whether an NDA / cooperation schedule is required.

### Do not promise
Funding approval, government endorsement, exclusivity, GCC purchase orders or Halal certification.

## Brief 02 — Manufacturer

### Objective
Determine readiness for onboarding into the China → GCC assurance corridor.

### Ask
1. Legal entity / beneficial ownership / facility address.
2. Product categories, SKUs, target GCC markets.
3. Ingredient/raw-material and supplier model.
4. Existing Halal certificates: issuer, scope, facility/product coverage, validity dates.
5. ERP/MES/QMS/WMS/LIMS/IoT/DMS stack.
6. Production line / batch genealogy capability.
7. Existing audit / CAPA history.
8. Laboratory process and sample custody.
9. Serialization / anti-counterfeit capability.
10. Willingness to support lot-level evidence and integration sandbox.

### Required output
Manufacturer onboarding dossier, facility readiness gaps, system inventory, nominated technical owner, candidate pilot SKU.

## Brief 03 — Laboratory

### Objective
Validate whether the laboratory can provide attributable scientific evidence into AHTE.

### Ask
- Exact registered legal entity.
- Accreditation body / accreditation number / current scope.
- Methods relevant to proposed product/material categories.
- Sample accession and custody procedure.
- QC, technical review and authorised signatory process.
- Amendment/reissue/versioning method.
- LIMS / API / SFTP / structured report capability.
- Data retention and security.
- Turnaround time and commercial schedule.

### Required output
Identity/accreditation evidence, method-scope matrix, chain-of-custody flow, technical integration owner, sample/result data dictionary.

### Hard boundary
`NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL`; laboratory evidence does not itself certify Halal status.

## Brief 04 — China traceability / serialization provider

### Objective
Map physical/digital product identity into AHTE without treating a scan or anti-counterfeit token as certification.

### Ask
- Exact legal operator and deployed platform.
- Item/lot/pallet/container identity model.
- QR/NFC/microdot/VOID or other physical carrier.
- Commission / aggregate / pack / ship / decommission / exception events.
- API, batch file, webhook, event stream or SFTP options.
- Signing, timestamps, offline reconciliation and key management.
- Per-code / platform / integration commercial model.

### Required output
Field map, event taxonomy, integration pattern, security owner and sandbox path.

## Brief 05 — Sinotrans

### Objective
Establish the exact operational and integration path for China-origin warehouse and logistics custody.

### Ask
1. Exact contracting entity.
2. Named warehouses/CFS sites and functions.
3. Candidate China origin lanes and GCC handover points.
4. WMS/TMS/Y2T/MIS/EDI/IoT platforms in scope.
5. Booking, pickup, receipt, storage, dispatch, loading, container/seal and POD events.
6. Vehicle / container / seal / route / temperature / geofence telemetry.
7. Subcontractor controls.
8. Incident/tamper/damage escalation.
9. API/EDI/SFTP/MQ availability and technical owner.
10. Liability, insurance, tariff and service-level contracting path.

### Required output
Site/lane matrix, system/interface owners, event field set, security requirements, commercial schedule inputs.

### Hard boundary
Sinotrans custody evidence supports assurance; it does not create Halal certification or sovereign customs release.

## Brief 06 — PHC / GHSCL internal mission governance

### Objective
Align every external meeting to one institutional position.

### Confirm before meetings
- who speaks for PHC;
- who speaks for GHSCL;
- who may discuss technical architecture;
- who may discuss commercial terms;
- who may sign a non-binding instrument;
- who may approve public use of names/logos;
- which claims are verified, proposed, source-locked or open-gate.

### Required output
Daily executive decision log and next-day priorities.

## Brief 07 — Port / customs technical discovery

### Objective
Understand permitted technical interface patterns without implying access or release authority.

### Ask
- authorized point of contact;
- data exchange channel;
- shipment/container/seal identifiers accepted;
- inspection/sampling/hold/release event structure;
- signed response / receipt method;
- authentication / PKI;
- sandbox/UAT process;
- security, retention and jurisdiction constraints.

### Required output
Adapter requirement record only.

### Hard boundary
Sovereign release remains with the port/customs authority.

---

## Brief 08 — GCC buyer/importer follow-up

### Objective
Validate receiving/import/vendor requirements for a real candidate SKU.

### Ask
- exact operating legal entity;
- vendor onboarding process;
- importer of record;
- product category / HS code expectations;
- required authority/certificate evidence;
- receiving DC / temperature / shelf-life requirements;
- packaging / label / language requirements;
- commercial buying process.

### Required output
Destination requirement checklist.

### Do not promise
Listing, purchase order, volume, margin, import approval or destination authority acceptance.
