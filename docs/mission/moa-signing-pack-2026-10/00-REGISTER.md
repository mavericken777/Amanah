## Signing-room control sequence

1. Confirm exact legal entity and authorised representative for every Party.
2. Confirm scope and strike any inapplicable clause/schedule deliberately; never leave contradictory alternatives.
3. Complete Annex A (operating scope) and Annex B (data/evidence/security).
4. Confirm whether Annex C is excluded or separately executed.
5. Resolve bilingual wording and controlling language.
6. Confirm no unsupported JAKIM, customs, laboratory, exclusivity, procurement or financing claim remains.
7. Produce execution PDF and SHA-256; record version/date.
8. Sign/date/seal as required; never backdate.
9. Store executed original privately; repository records only controlled metadata unless disclosure is authorised.
10. Update signing register with execution status, custody reference and follow-up obligations.
