# AMANAH China Mission Prompt Coverage Audit

**Date:** 7 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Test the previous 69-item mandate against the whole AMANAH / Global Halal Digital Trust platform and the actual China-trip presentation requirement.

## Conclusion

The previous mandate was broad, but it mixed three different things:

1. platform architecture;
2. internal engineering / repository execution;
3. external China-trip deliverables.

That made the work look fragmented and encouraged completion claims based on engineering files instead of finished presentation material.

The revised master prompt closes that problem by making the **finished China-mission delivery suite** the primary outcome and keeping engineering activity behind the scenes.

## Main change

The previous programme described **what should exist**.

The revised prompt requires **finished things the user can actually carry into meetings**:

- master deck;
- corporate profile;
- standards/JAKIM framework pack;
- MOA suite;
- Sinotrans playbook;
- CODA finance pack;
- laboratory pack;
- manufacturer pack;
- port/API pack;
- meeting brief book;
- objection-handling book;
- printed binder;
- website;
- infographic suite;
- cinematic video package;
- Mandarin adaptations.

## Standards completeness rule

The platform must never be hard-coded conceptually to MS 1500 and MS 2400, or even permanently to a fixed count.

The current verified primary Malaysian/JAKIM standards catalogue contains 17 standards, with additional applicable product/technical instruments such as MS 2683:2017. The architecture is registry-driven so additional verified Malaysian/JAKIM standards and authority instruments can be added without redesign.

The wider framework includes MPPHM 2020, MHMS 2020, HAS, IHCS, protocols, circulars, authority instructions, destination rules and laboratory methods.

## Presentation principle

External China-trip materials show the **finished platform, operating model, partnership proposition and evidence architecture**.

Internal engineering, branch history, CI mechanics, migrations, source-state labels and backlog language stay out of the main presentation package.
