## 5. Mission day discipline

### Before every meeting
- Verify exact legal entity and meeting participants.
- Identify requested decision and maximum acceptable outcome.
- Open the stakeholder-specific meeting brief.
- Carry the relevant corporate profile / architecture / infographic / MOA only.
- Mark every unverified statement as an assumption requiring validation.
- Do not present architecture as proof of a live production integration.

### During every meeting
Capture:
- names, titles, authority to commit;
- product/facility/site/system scope;
- documents/evidence shown;
- technical interface owner;
- commercial statements and whether indicative/binding;
- legal/signing path;
- open questions;
- explicit next actions and due dates.

### After every meeting — within 15 minutes
- Create/update meeting record.
- Record decisions separately from discussion.
- Create tasks with owner/date.
- Link documents/evidence.
- Update risks and open gates.
- Record what was promised by whom.
- Do not convert verbal discussion into “executed agreement”.

**Operating model:** GHSCL coordinates the international digital/operating layer; AHTE links standards, controls, evidence, digital twins, events and trust states; China-origin manufacturers/labs/logistics contribute attributable evidence; Sinotrans handles warehouse/logistics scope; GCC actors handle destination acceptance; competent authorities retain certification and sovereign decisions.

**Technology position:** AI assists review and risk detection; cryptographic controls protect integrity; IoT and smart audit improve observability; none of these independently certify Halal.
